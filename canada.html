📖 About Vanier Scholarships

The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships programme supports highly qualified doctoral students who demonstrate strong academic achievement, research potential and leadership qualities. The programme is designed to attract and retain outstanding doctoral researchers in Canada.

✅ Eligibility

  • 🎓 Applicants must meet the requirements for doctoral-level study.
  • 📚 Strong academic achievement and research potential are important.
  • 🔬 Applicants must have an eligible doctoral research programme.
  • 🇨🇦 Candidates must meet the programme's Canadian eligibility and nomination requirements.
  • 📄 Specific eligibility rules and nomination conditions apply.

🎁 Scholarship Benefits

  • 💰 Financial support is provided for eligible doctoral scholars.
  • 🎓 Support is intended for doctoral-level study and research in Canada.
  • 🔬 The scholarship recognises academic excellence, research potential and leadership.
  • 🌎 Provides an opportunity to conduct advanced research in Canada.

📄 Common Documents

  • 📄 Academic transcripts and records
  • 📄 Research proposal or research-related information
  • 📄 CV / Resume
  • 📝 Personal and leadership information, where required
  • 📑 Recommendation or reference information, where required
  • 📋 Additional documents requested by the nominating institution

📝 How to Apply

  • 01. 🔎 Check the current Vanier programme requirements.
  • 02. 🎓 Identify an eligible doctoral programme and institution.
  • 03. 📋 Review the nomination and application process.
  • 04. 📄 Prepare the required academic and research documents.
  • 05. 📅 Submit through the appropriate process before the official deadline.

📖 About Chevening

Chevening is the UK Government's international scholarship programme. It supports outstanding professionals from eligible countries and territories to study a one-year master's degree at a UK university.

✅ Eligibility

  • 🌍 Applicants must be citizens of a Chevening-eligible country or territory.
  • 🎓 Applicants must have completed an undergraduate degree that enables entry to a UK postgraduate programme.
  • 💼 Applicants need relevant work experience according to the current Chevening requirements.
  • 📝 Applicants must meet the programme's leadership and networking criteria.
  • 🏫 Applicants must meet the admission requirements of their chosen UK university courses.

🎁 Scholarship Benefits

  • 🎓 Tuition fees for an eligible master's programme.
  • 💰 Monthly living allowance.
  • ✈️ Travel costs to and from the UK may be covered.
  • 🏠 Support for eligible arrival and departure costs may be included.
  • 📋 Additional allowances may apply under the current scholarship terms.

📄 Common Documents

  • 📄 Academic certificates and transcripts
  • 📄 CV / professional information
  • 📝 Scholarship application essays
  • 📑 References, where required
  • 🪪 Passport or identity document
  • 🎓 UK university course choices and admission information

📝 How to Apply

  • 01. 🔎 Check whether you are eligible for the current Chevening cycle.
  • 02. 🎓 Research eligible one-year master's courses in the UK.
  • 03. 📄 Prepare your application and supporting information.
  • 04. 🖥️ Submit the Chevening application through the official online system.
  • 05. 📅 Follow the official timeline and complete university applications as required.
